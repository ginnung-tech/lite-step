# Lite-STEP Asset Bundle Notice & Provenance

This directory contains accompanying assets for Lite-STEP compiled IFC models (`model.ifc`), including the presentation shell (`model.blend`), the 2D drawing stylesheet (`styles/lite-step.css`), and PBR textures (`textures/`).

## Texture Provenance & License

All texture maps in `textures/` are sourced from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) under the [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

For full details on original author attribution, upstream URLs, asset slugs, and material mappings, see [`textures/NOTICE.md`](textures/NOTICE.md).
