# Texture Assets Notice & Provenance

All texture maps included in `lite_step/assets/textures/` are sourced from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) and are dedicated to the public domain under the [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

These assets are distributed in Lite-STEP at 1024×1024 resolution (Lanczos downscaled from the upstream 4K sources) to provide crisp PBR viewport presentation while keeping repository and bundle size lightweight (~2 MB total).
